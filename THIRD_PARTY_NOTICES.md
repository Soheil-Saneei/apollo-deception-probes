# Source attribution and reuse

Insider-trading transcripts, roleplaying scenarios/completions, grading prompts, and selected source files originate from [Apollo Research's deception-detection repository](https://github.com/ApolloResearch/deception-detection), pinned to commit `f8ec4010e74927394709dffa22b97bdf8cd5a62f`. See its linked paper, *Detecting Strategic Deception Using Linear Probes*, for authorship and upstream methods.

Apollo labels, source IDs, and provenance are retained. Our strict inclusion filter, frozen replay artifacts, generated-Qwen runs, probes, comparisons, and figures are additional work. This repository is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apollo Research.

No top-level license was found in the inspected upstream repository. Public availability is not a blanket license grant. No new license is asserted over Apollo's material; consult its authors for reuse terms beyond applicable permissions. This repository does not grant rights to third-party model weights or alter provider terms.

Model checkpoints are identified by provider and revision but excluded from downloads. Archived activations and probe weights are research artifacts, not base-model parameters.
